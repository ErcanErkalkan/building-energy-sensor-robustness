# Original data access and required paths

Source data come from their publishers and are not created by the manuscript author. Licence and provenance: `../DATA_LICENSE_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md`.

- Download the **UCI Appliances Energy Prediction** original archive from https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VC8G and place it at `code/UCI/data/uci_appliances_energy_original.zip` (relative to repository root).
- Download the **SmartlivingEPC** archives from https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15781077 and place the seven source ZIPs in `code/SmartlivingEPC/data_external/` with the exact source names, including `Single Family House.zip`, `Private flat.zip`, `School.zip`, and `Sports Center.zip`. The original seven-archive inventory is reported in `code/SmartlivingEPC/results/V14_all_seven_archive_inventory.csv`.

The original archives may also be mirrored in this repository, depending on GitHub upload and licence constraints. Downloading them from DOI records is the authoritative fallback. Exact file bytes/MD5 are audited by the included scripts and checksums.
