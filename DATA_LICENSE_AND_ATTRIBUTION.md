# Source datasets and attribution

This is a **public** research repository. The two underlying public datasets were published as **CC BY 4.0** by their respective source providers. The source archives, if mirrored here, retain their original licence and provenance. The author's analysis scripts and manuscript are distinct works and are not automatically relicensed as CC BY 4.0.

1. **Appliances Energy Prediction**. L. Candanedo (2017), UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VC8G. The target is appliance energy in Wh per 10-minute interval. The original source description mentions weather data supplied by an external service.
2. **SmartlivingEPC Building Complex real-time measurements**. A. Veliskaki and coauthors (2025), Zenodo. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15781077. This source project was supported by EU grant 101069639. **This manuscript itself received no external funding.**

Derived hourly meter increments, synthetic sensor-missingness masks and evaluation tables are analytical results. Synthetic mask patterns must not be described as observed sensor hardware failures.
